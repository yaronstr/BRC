Questions worth asking

Before you decide

How do you select surgeons?

We work with established English-speaking Barcelona surgeons and clinics relevant to each procedure. You will receive the surgeon's identity and credentials and speak with them directly before any decision. BRC does not make the clinical decision for you.

Is surgery abroad safe?

All surgery carries risk, whether at home or abroad. Travelling adds practical considerations including flights, distance from the surgeon and follow-up. Your surgeon must explain your individual risks and confirm when you are fit to travel. BRC's role is to reduce gaps in coordination—not to describe surgery as risk-free.

Who helps if I am worried after surgery?

BRC helps you contact the clinic promptly and arrange practical support. Administrative help with an extended stay or readmission carries no additional coordination fee; extra accommodation and services are charged separately. Medical assessment and treatment remain the responsibility of the surgeon, clinic or emergency services.

Can I travel alone?

Sometimes, but it depends on the procedure, mobility and the surgeon's protocol. Tell us early if you plan to travel alone so the clinical and practical support can be assessed properly.

Will I receive one all-inclusive price?

You will receive a clear proposal that distinguishes surgeon and clinic charges from BRC accommodation, support and optional services. We do not use countdown offers or pressure you to commit.
