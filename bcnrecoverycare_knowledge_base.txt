## ABOUT BCN RECOVERY CARE

BCN Recovery Care is a premium post-operative and aesthetic recovery service based in L'Hospitalet de Llobregat, Barcelona, Spain. We specialise in providing a safe, comfortable, and professionally supervised environment for patients recovering from aesthetic and cosmetic surgeries. Our service bridges the gap between hospital discharge and returning home — offering medical-grade care in a warm, home-like setting.

Our address is in L'Hospitalet de Llobregat, within minutes of major Barcelona clinics and hospitals.

## RECOVERY GUIDES BY PROCEDURE TYPE

### Rhinoplasty (Nose Surgery)
Typical recovery stay: 3–5 days

The first 48 hours after rhinoplasty are the most critical. Swelling and bruising around the nose and eyes are normal and peak around day 2–3. Patients must sleep with the head elevated at 30–45 degrees to reduce swelling. Cold compresses are applied around (never on) the nose. A nasal splint is worn for approximately one week.

At BCN Recovery Care we monitor for signs of excessive bleeding, breathing difficulty, or infection. We coordinate splint removal with your surgeon. Patients should avoid blowing their nose, wearing glasses, or any contact with the nose area for at least 3 weeks post-surgery.

Our care includes: head-elevation positioning, cold compress protocol, medication timing, nasal hygiene guidance, and liaison with your surgical team.

### Breast Augmentation
Typical recovery stay: 3–5 days

After breast augmentation patients experience tightness, soreness, and limited arm mobility for the first several days. Post-operative bra fitting and care is critical. Patients should avoid lifting arms above shoulder height for 2 weeks and any strenuous activity for 4–6 weeks.

We monitor for signs of hematoma, implant displacement, or infection. We assist with dressing changes, correct bra fitting, and sleeping position (slightly reclined for the first nights). Lymphatic drainage from day 3 helps reduce swelling and fibrosis.

### Breast Reduction / Mastopexy (Breast Lift)
Typical recovery stay: 4–6 days

These procedures involve more extensive incisions than augmentation, requiring careful wound management. Drains may be present for the first 1–2 days. We manage drain output, perform daily dressing changes, and monitor for seroma formation.

Lymphatic drainage massage begins once wounds are sufficiently healed (typically day 3–4). Compression is essential throughout recovery.

### Abdominoplasty (Tummy Tuck)
Typical recovery stay: 5–7 days

Abdominoplasty is one of the more demanding recoveries we manage. Patients spend the first days in a flexed position (slightly bent at the waist) to reduce tension on the abdominal closure. Walking in this position is gradually introduced from day 1.

Compression garments must be worn 24 hours a day for the first 4–6 weeks. We manage abdominal binders, monitor drain output, assist with mobility, and begin lymphatic drainage from day 2–3. Nutrition focuses on high protein and anti-inflammatory foods to support tissue healing.

### Liposuction
Typical recovery stay: 2–4 days (depending on extent)

Liposuction recovery involves significant swelling and bruising in treated areas. Compression garments are critical and must be worn continuously. Lymphatic drainage massage is one of the most important elements of liposuction recovery and is started as early as day 2 to reduce fibrosis (hardening of tissue) and accelerate visible results.

We offer daily lymphatic drainage sessions, compression garment management, and skin care in treated areas.

### Brazilian Butt Lift (BBL)
Typical recovery stay: 5–7 days

BBL recovery requires strict positioning protocols — patients must avoid sitting or lying directly on the buttocks for 2–6 weeks. We provide special BBL cushions and positioning guidance. This is one of the most positioning-dependent recoveries we manage.

Compression on the donor liposuction areas is also managed. Lymphatic drainage begins early. We provide 24/7 monitoring as BBL carries specific risks that require vigilant observation in the early post-operative period.

### Facelift (Rhytidectomy)
Typical recovery stay: 4–6 days

Facelift recovery requires careful bandage management, drain monitoring, and swelling control. Head elevation is maintained at all times. Cold therapy is applied carefully to reduce bruising and swelling. Patients must avoid any neck tension or sudden movements.

We assist with gentle facial hygiene, hair washing guidance, and preparation for suture/staple removal.

### Blepharoplasty (Eyelid Surgery)
Typical recovery stay: 3–4 days

Eyelid surgery results in significant bruising and swelling around the eyes for the first week. Cold compresses, prescribed eye drops, and head elevation are central to recovery. We monitor for dry eyes, excessive tearing, or any visual changes that would require immediate medical attention.

### Combined Procedures (e.g. Mummy Makeover)
Typical recovery stay: 7–10 days

Patients undergoing multiple procedures simultaneously (e.g. breast augmentation + abdominoplasty + liposuction) require extended and more intensive care. Recovery protocols are combined and carefully sequenced. Pain management is more complex and is coordinated closely with the surgical team.

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

**Do I need a referral from my surgeon?**
No. You can book directly with us. However, we always coordinate with your surgical team to ensure continuity of care.

**Can a family member stay with me?**
Yes. Our Family Companion Suite is designed exactly for this. One companion can stay with you throughout your recovery.

**What languages do you speak?**
Susana and the team speak Spanish, Catalan, and English. We can arrange translation support for other languages.

**Are you a medical clinic?**
We are a supervised recovery residence, not a hospital or clinic. We provide nursing-level care but are not equipped for surgical emergencies. In the event of a serious complication, we coordinate immediate transfer to hospital.

**Can I have visitors?**
Yes, with some restrictions during the first 24–48 hours when rest is paramount. We ask that visitors respect quiet hours (10pm–9am).

**Do you offer lymphatic drainage massage?**
Yes. We have a certified lymphatic drainage therapist who visits regularly. Sessions are included in some packages or can be added individually.

**What should I bring?**
We provide most essentials (linens, towels, basic toiletries). We recommend bringing: comfortable loose clothing that opens at the front, your prescribed medications, your pre-op documents, phone charger, and any personal comfort items.

**How do I book?**
Contact us by phone, WhatsApp, or the contact form on our website. We recommend booking at least 2 weeks before your procedure date, especially during high season (spring and summer).
